I am an undergraduate student studying B Tech Ed. IT at Kathmandu University (Radiant College). My research interest lies in the areas of operating systems, computer architecture, and secure & trustworthy systems, with a focus on hardware/software interfaces and low-level system vulnerabilities.

I have completed rigorous online coursework from top institutions including MIT 6.S081 (Operating System Engineering), MIT 6.033 (Computer Systems Engineering), CMU CS15-213 (Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective), and UC Berkeley CS61C (Computer Architecture). Before joining KU, I completed my Vocational & Technical Education in Civil Engineering (Grades 9–12) at Pharsatikar Secondary School, Nepal.

I am actively seeking research mentorship, graduate opportunities, and collaborations in computer systems and architecture security:
  • Systems & OS Security: Kernel design, xv6 enhancements, virtual memory, and hardware-assisted isolation.
  • Architecture: Micro-architectural side-channels, transient execution security, and cache timing analysis.
  • Background: C/C++, Rust, Assembly, Python, Linux kernel internals, and low-level debugging (GDB).
